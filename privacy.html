Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how TikTok Auto Post handles information when users connect their TikTok account and use the application's publishing features.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, the application may process information required by TikTok's official APIs, including TikTok account identifiers, authorization tokens, video files, captions, and publishing status information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to authenticate the user, upload or publish requested content, and provide the application's publishing functionality.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the requested functionality through TikTok's official APIs or where required by law.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's functionality, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. User Choices

Users may stop using the application and revoke its authorization through TikTok's account and application settings.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the developer account.
